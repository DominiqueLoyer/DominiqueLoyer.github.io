Modeling a Hybrid System (Predicate-Logic-Based + ML/AI) for Verifying the Credibility of Information Sources
Author Dominique S. Loyer
Year 2025
Type Preprint
DOI 10.13140/RG.2.2.36348.24961
Status Under Review
This paper presents a neuro-symbolic approach combining predicate-logic ontologies with machine learning techniques for assessing information source credibility. The hybrid system leverages domain knowledge encoded in logical predicates with the adaptive learning capabilities of neural networks.
